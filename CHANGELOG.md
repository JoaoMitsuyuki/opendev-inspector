## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-23

### Added

- Modular CLI with terminal and JSON reports.
- Local scanners for secrets, configuration, dependencies, and quality.
- Masked secret evidence and bounded, symlink-safe file discovery.
- Severity-based exit policy with `--fail-on`.
- English and Portuguese documentation, tests, and cross-platform CI configuration.
