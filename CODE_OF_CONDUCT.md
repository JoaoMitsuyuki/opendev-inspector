# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Be constructive, assume good intent, respect privacy and boundaries, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sexualized conduct are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement contact details will be added before the repository becomes public. Reports will be reviewed privately and fairly.
